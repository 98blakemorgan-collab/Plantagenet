Author’s Notes
Taking panto from Page to Stage...
Year-round Pantomimes
Panto is most popular during December and the Christmas season; however, more and 
more productions are taking place at other times of year. If required you are permitted to 
change seasonal Christmas references to something more seasonably appropriate.
The Cue-Card Scene (Scene Six)
Ariel communicates with Evan using a stack of large hand-lettered cards in a homage to 
the same scene in the film Love Actually. The underscore music is flexible, "Silent Night" is 
used in the film, but any similar music works too. Don’t forget to make the cards large 
enough to be clearly readable by those on the back row!
The Voice-Passing Routine (Scene Eight)
In this scene, Ariel's voice, released from the shell, passes between characters as a 
spotlight moves between them. The actor playing Ariel speaks the lines from offstage while 
each character lip-syncs in turn. The spotlights can be preset from above, with specific 
places for the actors to stand beneath them, or a follow-spot can flick between them: 
whatever works best for you!

Act One
Prologue: Beneath the Waves
TABS CLOSED
There is a magical sound as THE SPIRIT OF THE SEA enters. She addresses 
the AUDIENCE.
SPIRIT
Good evening, dear friends, and welcome to you,
It’s time for our panto, there’s lots to get through.
I’m the Spirit of the Sea, and I’ve come here tonight,
To set the scene properly, and start our tale right.
Deep beneath the surface, under the sea,
There is a kingdom of wonders, but it’s not Disney,
We have to be different, so have added some twists,
Or we might get in trouble with the copyright-ists!
Our story retains the undersea realm,
Queen Marina is monarch and steadies the helm.
Her trident holds power and she sits on the throne,
But her sister is jealous and wants it for her own.
There is an evil chord. OCTAVIA enters. The AUDIENCE boo.
OCTAVIA
(Savouring the boos) I knew you lot would start early on the boos!
SPIRIT
This is Octavia, she’s a Sea-Witch,
She’s the Queen’s sister and a bit of a… handful.
She longs for the trident and covets the crown,
She’s making her plans to bring the Queen down.
OCTAVIA
Not just the Queen, I want to get rid of them all, and Ariel most of all!
SPIRIT
Ariel’s a princess, Marina’s first daughter,
She’s heir to the throne and all underwater,
But that is not all, she’s also been blessed,
With a voice that, quite simply, is the very best.
OCTAVIA
(To the AUDIENCE) It’s just a shame such a talent has been wasted on such 
a silly princess. That voice should be mine, and the trident too!
SPIRIT
Oh please stop your plotting, you know you can’t win,
Besides, it’s now time for our show to begin,
You can’t have her voice, it wouldn’t be fair,
Now off you go, go back to your lair.
OCTAVIA
Fine, but this isn’t the last you’ve heard of me!

Scene Two: The Singing Lesson
TABS CLOSED
DAME BELUGA enters. She sees the AUDIENCE and is immediately delighted.
DAME
Hello and welcome. Look at you all! Are you enjoying yourselves?
The AUDIENCE responds.

The houselights come up.
